Preset determinism (PRD §7 M2, §8.2)

Two checks, each rendered through an OfflineAudioContext:

  1. Per-preset restore. For every one of the 6 launch presets: apply it, render; then on a fresh engine apply it, mutate its 4 exposed knobs, re-apply it, and render again. If applyPreset() is exhaustive, the two waveforms must be identical.
  2. Cross-preset switching. Apply all 6 presets in sequence on ONE engine, ending back on the first preset, then render. Compare against a fresh engine that only ever applied that first preset. This is the multi-voicing, multi-pedal version of the leakage check above — it's what M2's "switching among all 6 presets is ... fully deterministic" acceptance criterion actually exercises.

Notes: the noise gate is held open during offline rendering (no requestAnimationFrame). The compressor pedal is forced OFF for every render because Chromium's DynamicsCompressorNode is itself non-deterministic in OfflineAudioContext (it differs between two identical renders) — testing it would test the browser, not our engine. Everything else in the chain (preamp waveshapers, tonestack filters, power-amp soft clip, pedal DSP incl. fuzz/leslie, gains, IR mix) renders bit-identically.
